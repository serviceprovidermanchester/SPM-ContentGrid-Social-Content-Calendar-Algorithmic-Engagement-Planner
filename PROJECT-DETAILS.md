# ContentGrid — Social Content Calendar & Engagement Planner

**Service:** Digital Product & Ads Management (Series 09, product 7 of 10)
**Built by:** SPManchester — https://spmanchester.com/

## Portfolio summary

ContentGrid answers the question social teams usually guess at: which
format and which time slot actually earn engagement? It scores every
published post with one documented model — (likes + 3×comments +
4×shares + 2×saves) ÷ reach × 100 — rolls the scores up by format and
by weekday × daypart slot, and audits the forward calendar against the
brand's own cadence and format-mix targets: posts per week vs target,
gap days, and mix percentages vs target.

For the showcase Northern Quarter Hospitality Group (Manchester), the
engine shows Reel leading at 9.33, Wednesday evening as the best slot
at 11, and a November calendar at 85% coverage (17 of 20 target posts,
11 gap days) — every figure verified by 62 live tests including 30
hand-computed expected-output cases.

**Stack:** zero-dependency Node.js backend (node:http / node:sqlite /
node:crypto), SQLite (11 tables), vanilla SPA (10 views), 28 REST
endpoints, PIL-rendered screenshots.

**Data honesty:** all platform data is a seeded local dataset for a
fictional business; live Meta/TikTok/LinkedIn API connections are the
documented production backlog, not claimed here.
