# Code of Conduct — SPManchester Community

SPManchester Private Limited is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all contributors and participants.

## Community Standards

We encourage behaviors that foster a positive collaborative atmosphere:
- Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward other contributors.
- Respecting differing viewpoints, technical opinions, and levels of experience.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the open-source community and project users.
- Maintaining professional communication in all issues, pull requests, and discussions.

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, derogatory comments, offensive language, trolling, personal attacks, or sharing others' private information without consent.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to:
- **Contact:** info@spmanchester.com
- **Organization:** SPManchester Private Limited

All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated confidentially.
